RESPECTING CREATIVE RIGHTS

Copyright / DMCA Policy.

We respect the rights of creators and take properly submitted copyright concerns seriously.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Copyright ownership

Unless a page states otherwise, wallpapers, artwork, photographs, logos and other creative material belong to their respective owners. Superwallpapers does not claim ownership of third-party material simply because it is displayed or linked on this website.

We aim to publish only material that we own, are authorised to use, or otherwise have a lawful basis to display. If you submit material for publication through an administrator account, you must have the rights and permissions needed to provide it for that use.

2. Submit a copyright complaint

If you believe material on Superwallpapers infringes your copyright, email us at contact@example.com with the subject "Copyright Removal Request". Please include:

  1. Your name and a way to contact you.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed. If there are several works, provide a representative list.
  3. The exact URL of the material on Superwallpapers that you want us to review.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, where you are acting for a rights holder, that you are authorised to act on that person's behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature or other reliable indication that you are making the request.

3. What happens after a report

We will review notices that contain enough information to identify the material and the rights concern. Where appropriate, we may remove or disable access to the reported material while we investigate, contact the relevant submitter, or take other action. Sending a notice does not guarantee a particular outcome or response time.

4. Counter-notices and mistaken reports

If material was removed because of a copyright report and you believe that removal was a mistake, contact us with the relevant page URL, an explanation and information showing why you believe you have the right to use the material. A formal legal counter-notice may require specific statements and personal information and can have legal consequences. Consider obtaining qualified legal advice before sending one.

5. Repeat infringement

We may restrict publishing access or take other appropriate action where an account repeatedly submits material that is reasonably identified as infringing copyright.

6. False or incomplete notices

Please submit reports only when you have a genuine rights concern and provide accurate information. False or misleading copyright claims can have legal consequences.

7. Contact

Copyright and DMCA requests should be sent to contact@example.com.

This page is a general website notice, not legal advice. It does not by itself register a designated copyright agent or guarantee compliance with every law that may apply.
