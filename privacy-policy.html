Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit or use Superwallpapers.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Superwallpapers ("we", "us" or "our") operates this website. This policy describes information that may be collected or processed through the website and how it is used. By using the website, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Information you provide

If you create an account or use account features, we may process information you submit, such as your email address and username. If you save favourites, the website stores the related wallpaper and account identifiers so that the feature can work.

When an administrator publishes a wallpaper, the image and the information supplied with it, such as its title, category and description, may be stored and displayed on the website.

2. Technical information

When you visit a website, hosting, security and service providers may automatically process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service. Depending on the provider, this may include an IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested and timestamps.

3. How information is used

  • To operate the website and display wallpaper pages.
  • To create and maintain accounts and authentication sessions.
  • To provide favourites, downloads and administrator publishing features.
  • To respond to support, feedback and copyright requests.
  • To help maintain website security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot errors.

4. Service providers and external services

Superwallpapers uses third-party services to operate parts of the website. These may include GitHub Pages for website hosting and Supabase for authentication, database and image-storage functions. The website also loads web fonts from Google Fonts and may display externally hosted demonstration images. Those providers may receive technical information when your browser connects to their services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to their services.

5. Cookies, browser storage and advertising

Authentication services may use browser storage or similar technologies to maintain your sign-in session. Superwallpapers also stores recent search terms and recently viewed wallpaper identifiers in your browser's local storage so those lists can be shown when you return. This browsing history is stored in that browser on that device and is not synced to your account by this feature. You can clear recent searches from the Search screen and clear browser site data through your browser settings. Your browser may also store other information required for website functionality.

If third-party advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to display ads, measure ad performance, limit repeated ads and, where permitted by your settings and applicable rules, personalise advertising.

If Google advertising services such as Google AdSense are used on this website, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this website and/or other websites. You can manage Google's personalised advertising settings at Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about Google's use of information on partner sites at How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps. Some third-party advertising cookies may also be managed through YourAdChoices.

When an advertising provider is selected and enabled, this policy should be updated to identify that provider and link to its privacy and opt-out information.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell account information as part of the website's normal operation. Information may be processed by the service providers described above to provide their services, or disclosed when required by law, to protect the website and its users, or to address security and rights-related concerns.

7. Data retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant features, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security. We use third-party service providers, so information may also be subject to their retention and security practices. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to create an account. You may contact us to ask about account information associated with your email address or to request correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements and records we may need to retain. You can also control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some storage may affect sign-in and other features.

9. External links

The website may link to third-party websites or services. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information to them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website's features, service providers or advertising arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@example.com.

This is a starter website privacy notice, not legal advice. Review and update it whenever you add analytics, advertising providers, consent tools or other services that process visitor information.
